Good question

Before you
take a bite.

A few useful answers. Check the individual product page for the exact flavour, nutrition panel and allergen details.

I don’t go to the gym. Are protein bars for me?

The official protein-bar page includes this question in its FAQ. Protein bars are a convenient snack option; whether one suits your routine depends on your own dietary needs and the product’s nutrition information.

How much protein is there in a bar?

It depends on the range and flavour. The standard Double Cocoa bar is listed as 12g protein per 52g bar; the Protein Bars Pro range is positioned at 20g protein per bar. Check the pack for the exact nutrition facts.

Is the protein vegetarian?

The Double Cocoa and Pro bars discussed here contain whey protein, a milk-derived ingredient. Check each product’s ingredients and dietary information before buying.

I’m lactose intolerant. Is whey okay for me?

Whey is milk-derived. Please check the product’s allergen information and consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether it is suitable for you.

Do the bars have sugar?

The Double Cocoa product page says “no added refined sugar” and lists dates among the ingredients. That does not mean the product contains zero naturally occurring sugars. Refer to the nutrition panel for total sugars.

Who makes these bars?

The Whole Truth’s official manufacturing page lists snack-bar manufacturing facility details, facility codes and FSSAI licence numbers. You can use the code on your pack to identify the relevant facility.
